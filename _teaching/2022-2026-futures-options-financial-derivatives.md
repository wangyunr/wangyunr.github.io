**Spring 2022, Spring 2024, Fall 2024, Spring 2026**

AEM 4210/6410 covers futures, options, hedging, and commodity risk management, with applications to energy and agricultural commodity markets.

## Teaching Responsibilities

- Helped design market based practice problems linking futures spreads, geopolitical risk premiums, and price volatility to energy spread trading strategies across crude oil and natural gas markets.

- Assisted in developing applied commodity market case studies using real futures and spot price data to analyze how supply and demand shocks, basis movements, and inventory conditions affect hedging decisions and commodity price risk management across metals, grains, and dairy markets.

- Instructed students on options and derivatives risk management, including strategy payoffs, the Greeks, Delta hedging, and Delta Vega neutral portfolios using Excel based modeling.
